The Thakur Shri Hanuman Ji Maharaj Govind Bag Samiti Trust is a non-profit, spiritual organization dedicated to the service (seva) of the sacred Shri Sitaram Kuteer, home to the ever-present Lord Hanuman, in the holy land of Vrindavan.

The Essence of Seva

"True service (seva) is not merely an act, but a state of being. It is the selfless offering of one's thoughts, words, and deeds at the feet of the divine, without expectation of reward..."

Finding Peace in Chaos

"In a world of constant noise, the name of the Lord is our unwavering anchor. Chanting His name calms the turbulent mind and reveals the profound peace that resides within each of us..."
